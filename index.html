about_project

A Clicker Inside an OS

Tea.run runs on LeafOS, a fake totaly real operating system built just for making tea. Start by clicking to gather leaves, unlock network upgrades in the browser, and eventually automate your entire production line.

Leaf Factory

The starting point. Harvest leaves manually before buying machines to do the heavy lifting for you.

Water Supply

Unlock and manage your water input. You can't brew anything without a steady stream of water.

Tea factory

Combine your gathered leaves and water to start producing actual tea and scale up your numbers.

TeaNet Browser

Use the in-game browser to access the network, buy upgrades, and track your global production stats.
