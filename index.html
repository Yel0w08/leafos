// what is this

A clicker inside a fake OS

Tea.run is an idle clicker game running on LeafOS — a fictional operating system dedicated to tea production. Click leaves, unlock factories, navigate TeaNet through Surfy, and scale your operation from a single bush to an industrial tea empire.

🍃

Leaf Factory

Your starting point. Click to harvest leaves, unlock auto-collectors, and scale your raw material output.

💧

Water Factory

Unlock water production to fuel the brewing process. A key resource on the path to industrial tea.

☕

Tea Factory

Combine leaves and water to brew tea at scale. Upgrade your production line via the TeaNet portal.

🌐

Surfy / TeaNet

An in-game browser giving access to the TeaNet — a network of factory dashboards and unlock nodes.

// production chain

How it works

Every cup of tea starts with a click. Scale up through the production chain to automate your empire.
